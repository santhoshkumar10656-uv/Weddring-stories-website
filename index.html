Meet the Storyteller

Siva Kumar Vadamalayan

Professional Photographer · Founder, Weddring Stories

Every celebration is a story that deserves to be told honestly — and beautifully.

I am Siva Kumar Vadamalayan, a professional photographer and the founder of Weddring Stories. I believe the finest photographs are never forced — they live in stolen glances, nervous laughter, quiet blessings and the spaces between the rituals.

From traditional temple weddings and golden-hour pre-wedding shoots to bridal portraits, birthdays, kids and celebrations of every kind, my work blends candid emotion with cinematic light — so that years from now you won’t just remember the day, you will feel it again.
